## About me

I am a Principal Research Scientist and the founder of the Computational and Mathematical Sciences (CMS) group at SRI International. My team and I work at the intersection of algorithm development and artificial intelligence (AI). My research focuses on the scientific foundations of AI and its applications to challenging computational problems in classical and quantum settings. I particularly enjoy working at the interface of multiple disciplines and finding common themes and connections. Prior to joining SRI International, I spent 15 years at Raytheon/United Technologies Research Center (RTRC/UTRC), ultimately serving as a Technical Fellow (the highest technical rank). I earned my Ph.D. in January 2008 from Cornell University, where I was a McMullen Fellow and won the H.D. Block Teaching Award. I received my master's and bachelor's degrees in Aerospace Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay in 2002.
